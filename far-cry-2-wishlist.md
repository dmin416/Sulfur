# Far Cry 2 — mods and changes wanted

From the November 2026 wishlist.

## Mods and changes

1. **Realistic damage** — no bullet sponges; keep toughness as realistic as the engine allows.
2. **Hit locations** — separate hitboxes for head, torso, and limbs, each with different damage. Jackal Mod model: pistols and SMGs kill with 1 bullet to the head, 2 to the torso, and 3 to arms, legs, hands, or feet. The Desert Eagle needs 2 to the limbs.
3. **Per-caliber / per-weapon damage.**
4. **Silent machete takedowns.**
5. **Weapon inspection** — hold R, using G.O.R.E as the reference.
6. **Lower perception** for assassination targets and snipers.
7. **Dan's Blood and Gore** — longer-lasting bullet-hole decals on bodies, larger blood spurts, higher decal limits.
8. **Jackal Mod** (https://www.moddb.com/mods/far-cry-2-jackal-mod) — the hit-location model above, plus rearmed AI, roving patrols that attack guard posts, unlocked weapons, and realistic ammo counts.
9. **FC Ballistics Pro** (optional) — per-weapon damage, recoil, weight, rate of fire, and effective distance.
10. **ModDB’s “An Almost Complete Guide to Far Cry 2 Modding”** — especially the `HitLocations` section for per-body-part damage.
